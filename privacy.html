🌸 Vivaah Ledger — Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

1. Introduction

Vivaah Ledger ("the App") is a wedding budget and guest management tool. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our application at vivaah-ledger.pages.dev.

2. Information We Collect

When you sign in with Google, we access:

  • Google account email address — to identify your account
  • Google Sheets & Drive access — to save and sync your wedding ledger data across devices

We do not access your Gmail, contacts, calendar, or any other Google services.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • Your email is used solely to identify your account and sync your ledger data
  • Your Google Sheets data is stored in your own Google account — we do not store it on our servers
  • We do not sell, share, or transfer your data to any third parties

4. Data Storage

All your wedding ledger data (vendors, guests, budgets, payments) is stored in:

  • Your browser's local storage (on your device)
  • A Google Sheet in your own Google Drive (when signed in)

We do not maintain any database of user data on our servers.

5. Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services:

  • Google Sign-In & Sheets API — for authentication and data sync
  • Cloudflare Pages — for hosting the application
  • EmailJS — for sending access approval notifications

6. Data Security

Access to the App is protected by an email approval gate — only users approved by the owner can access the application. All data transmission uses HTTPS encryption.

7. Your Rights

You can:

  • Sign out at any time to revoke the App's access to your Google account
  • Delete your Google Sheet to remove all synced data
  • Request removal of your access approval from the app owner

8. Contact

For any privacy concerns, contact us at: horizoneworld@gmail.com

About Vivaah Ledger

Vivaah Ledger is a private wedding management application built for Indian weddings. It helps families plan, budget, and coordinate their wedding events in one place. The app uses Google Sign-In to securely sync your wedding data to your own Google Sheet, so your data is always yours and accessible from any device.


The application requests access to Google Sheets and Google Drive solely to save and sync your wedding ledger data. We never access your Gmail, contacts, or any other Google services.
